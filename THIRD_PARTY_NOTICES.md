## Audio and fireworks
- The mod reads audio from **user-installed SongCore custom-song folders** at runtime; no installed custom-song audio is redistributed.
- The mod attempts to reuse Beat Saber's native fireworks effects, and does not bundle the game's proprietary effects or audio.
- `FileCopies/Midnight_Synthwave_Tokyo.ogg` is an unused development asset, **not included in the packaged QMOD**. Its original source and redistribution permission should be reviewed before including it in any release asset or source archive.

The release QMOD does not contain Beat Saber's original menu soundtrack or a third-party music playlist.
